What Budgy does

Add transactions in seconds, categorise them and see your budget in real time.

📄Bills & QR-bills

Scan Swiss QR bills, keep due dates in sight and get payment reminders.

🔁Recurring costs

Set up rent, health insurance and subscriptions once – Budgy carries them over every month.

🎯Savings goals

Define goals (holidays, emergency fund, big purchases) and watch your progress grow.

📊Statistics

Monthly charts, category breakdown and 12-month trends.

🇨🇭Built for Switzerland

Why use a budget app?

Between cards, mobile payments and online shopping, it's easy to lose track of where money goes. People who record their spending consistently spend more consciously, spot unused subscriptions and reach savings goals faster. Budgy makes tracking so quick it becomes a habit – it also works offline, is protected by Face ID and shows no ads.

Frequently asked questions

Is Budgy free?

Yes. All essential features (transactions, budgets, bills, statistics) are free. An optional Pro subscription unlocks advanced features such as the unlimited AI coach.

Is my financial data safe?

Your data lives on your device and, if you enable sync, is stored encrypted in Europe. No ads, no data selling.

Which devices are supported?

Budgy is available for iPhone and iPad (iOS 15+) on the App Store. An Android version is planned.
